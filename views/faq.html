Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I pause or delete my alerts whenever I want?

Yes. From your dashboard you can activate, pause, edit, or delete any alert with a single click and cancel your subscription whenever you wish.

Are vehicles purchased directly on the platform?

No. CarsAlert acts as a monitoring and notification system; each alert directs you to the original listing so you can deal directly with the seller.

Want to buy the car, but don't know how?

We work with professional importers who can contact you to help import your vehicle and guide you through the process.

How are my data and privacy handled under the GDPR?

Your data is collected in accordance with the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and may be shared with or used by third parties for commercial or business purposes.
